# Getting started

Solo Remover removes your own instrument from a recording so you can play along. You need a free MVSEP account once, then everything runs in your browser.

## 1. Get a free MVSEP key (about 2 minutes, once)

MVSEP is the free online service that separates the instruments. The app sends your recording to it with your own key.

1. Go to [mvsep.com](https://mvsep.com) and choose **Create Account**. Confirm your email if asked, then **Log in**.
2. In the top menu choose **API** (the page is [mvsep.com/user-api](https://mvsep.com/user-api)). Copy the key shown there.
3. If you have used MVSEP's API before, press the button on that page to create a **new** key.

Keep the key private, like a password. Anyone with it can use your MVSEP quota.

## 2. Open the app and paste the key

1. Open the app (the web version: https://mimreta.github.io/solo-remover-web/). On a phone you can use **Add to Home screen**.
2. Go to the **Settings** tab, paste the key into the box and press **Save**. It stays in this browser only.

## 3. Make your first accompaniment

1. On the first tab, choose a recording (MP3, WAV, FLAC or M4A) you have the right to use.
2. Choose **your** instrument, the one to remove.
3. Press the main button and wait. A long recording is processed in parts; on the free plan the queue can be slow, and you can cancel any time.
4. When it says it is ready, the player opens. Press play and play your instrument in the empty place.

## 4. Practice tools (all optional)

Loop a section (drag on the waveform), name markers, slow down gradually, change tuning, use the metronome, open your own PDF part, and record yourself (use headphones). Download the accompaniment as MP3 or WAV before you close the page: results live only in the open page.

## If something goes wrong

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Add your API key in Settings first" | Do step 2 above. |
| "invalid token" | The key is wrong or was reset. Copy a fresh key from mvsep.com/user-api. |
| Long wait or "queue" | The free MVSEP plan has a queue. Try again later. |
| Microphone blocked | Allow the microphone for the site in the browser's address bar. |
| "Library did not load" | Check your internet connection and retry. |
