# Contributing

Thanks for wanting to help. This is a small personal project, so keep changes focused.

- **Bugs and ideas:** open an issue and describe what you did, what you expected and what happened. Never paste your MVSEP key or your recordings.
- **Pull requests:** one change per PR, short English commit messages. Say how you tested it (browser, phone width, light and dark).
- **Design:** the UI follows one design system (navy and teal, Vazirmatn, RTL, one column, three tabs). Please keep new UI consistent and check it at 320 px width.
- **Privacy rules:** no analytics, no tracking, no personal data, no real recordings or copyrighted audio in the repo, no new service that receives user audio other than MVSEP.
- **Licenses:** every dependency must be compatible with MIT. Add it to THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
- **Code header:** `Copyright (c) 2026 MohammadReza Tayebi` on new source files.

By contributing you agree that your contribution is released under the MIT license.
